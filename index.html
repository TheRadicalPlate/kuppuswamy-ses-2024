Methodological Reference

Income thresholds are calibrated to the Consumer Price Index for Industrial Workers (CPI-IW) as updated by:
Ahmad, A., Faizi, N., & Siddiqui, N. A. (2024). Revised Prasad and Kuppuswamy socioeconomic scales for India in 2024: Methods and Updates. Indian Journal of Community Health, 36(4), 598–603. DOI: 10.47203/IJCH.2024.v36i04.015 (CC BY-NC-ND 4.0).
